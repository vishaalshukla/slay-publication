Questions

Before you write to us

Do you guarantee publication?

No honest publisher can. We guarantee the manuscript will meet the target journal's standard and that we stay with you through revisions and resubmission at no extra fee.

Is this ghostwriting?

No. You supply the research and the argument; we develop, edit and format it. Every deliverable is reviewed and approved by you before it moves forward.

How long does journal publication take?

Eight to twenty weeks for most Scopus and Web of Science journals, depending on the review cycle. Book publication runs six to ten weeks from final manuscript.

What does it cost?

Fees are scoped per project after the consultation. Editing engagements start lower than full writing engagements; book publication is quoted by page extent and print run.

Do you handle non-English manuscripts?

Yes — translation and language editing for submissions originally drafted in Hindi, Arabic, Spanish or French.
