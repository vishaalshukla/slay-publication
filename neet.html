Frequently Asked Questions

How is the mentorship program different from regular coaching?

Unlike coaching classes that follow a fixed curriculum for all, our mentorship is 1-to-1 and fully personalized. A doctor-mentor works with you individually, understands your weak areas, and builds a strategy specifically for you.

Can I choose my mentor?

Yes. After your free counselling session, you'll be matched with a mentor based on your subject needs and goals. You can also request a specific mentor if you have a preference.

What is the duration of the program?

Programs range from 3 months to 12 months depending on your NEET timeline. We offer flexible plans for dropper students, Class 11 & 12 students, and repeaters.

Is there any trial session?

Yes — your first counselling session is completely free. It includes a diagnostic assessment and a personalized study roadmap at no cost.

How do I track my progress?

You'll receive weekly performance reports, mock test analysis, and monthly review calls with your mentor to track improvement and adjust your plan.

What if I miss a session?

All sessions are recorded and shared with you. Missed sessions can be rescheduled within the same week at no extra charge.
